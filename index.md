# Release Radar Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Release Radar is a Jira Rovo agent that helps authorized users assess release
readiness. It produces a read-only risk summary for a Jira project and the exact
release version requested by the user.

## Information processed

When a user runs an analysis, Release Radar accesses Jira project and version
details and unresolved issue information needed for the report. This can include
issue keys, summaries, statuses, priorities, due dates, update dates, issue links,
and whether an issue has an assignee. Jira content may contain personal
information. Release Radar does not include assignee identity in its report.

The app accesses Jira through the invoking user's Atlassian account and is
limited by that user's Jira permissions and the `read:jira-work` scope.

## How information is used

The information is used only to calculate and return an explainable release-risk
summary, including risk indicators, linked blockers, and suggested next steps.
The app does not create, edit, or delete Jira data.

The request is processed by Atlassian Forge and Rovo to provide the app's
functionality. The app does not send Jira data to external services.

## Storage and retention

Release Radar does not maintain its own database or persist Jira issue data.
Information remains in the Atlassian services that provide Jira, Forge, and Rovo,
and is subject to their applicable privacy, security, and retention practices.
